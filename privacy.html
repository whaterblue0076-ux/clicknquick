Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how it's used.

Last updated: 2026.

What we collect

To show you the right local store, we detect your approximate country from your IP address using a third-party lookup service (ipapi.co) when you visit. We don't store this — it's used only to pick which store (Daraz, Amazon, or AliExpress) to route you to on that visit.

If you use the wishlist (heart) button, the list of items you've saved is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) — it stays on your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else.

If you use our contact form, it opens your own email app with a pre-filled message — we only see what you choose to send us by email.

What we don't do

We don't run ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other websites. We don't require an account or login to browse the site.

Third parties

When you click a "View Deal" button, you leave our site and go to the retailer (Daraz, Amazon, or AliExpress). Their own privacy policy applies once you're there — we don't control what they collect.

Questions

Reach out any time on our Contact page.
