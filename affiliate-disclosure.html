Affiliate Disclosure

How Click & Quick makes money, in plain language.

Click & Quick participates in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program and Daraz's affiliate network (via DCMnetwork). This means that when you click a "View Deal" link on this site and go on to make a purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. The price you pay is the same whether you come through our link or not.

How this affects what we show you

We only feature products we believe offer genuine value. We label every price as either "verified" (we've personally confirmed it against the live retailer listing) or, if not, we say so plainly rather than showing a guessed number. We don't invent discounts or reviews to make a product look better than it is.

What we don't do

We're not paid by brands to write positive reviews, and a product being on this site doesn't mean the manufacturer endorses or is affiliated with Click & Quick in any other way.

Questions

If anything on a product page seems unclear or you spot an error, let us know on our Contact page.
